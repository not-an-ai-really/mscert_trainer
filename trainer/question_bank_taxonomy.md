### Difficulty Levels
- **Easy**: Foundational knowledge, factual recall
- **Medium**: Application of concepts, basic analysis
- **Hard**: Complex case scenarios, synthesis, evaluation

### Reasoning Structure for Each Question
1. **Stem**: Present clinical scenario or concept.
2. **Options**: Provide one correct answer and plausible distractors.
3. **Correct Answer**: Specify the right option.
4. **Rationale**: Explain why the correct answer is appropriate, referencing evidence or guideline.
