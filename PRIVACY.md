# Privacy Policy — MS-Cert Trainer

**Last updated: 17 September 2026**

## Summary

MS-Cert Trainer collects no data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no
analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party code of any
kind. The app makes no network requests once installed.

## What is stored, and where

The app stores one thing: your practice progress — a running count of
questions answered and answered correctly, broken down by content domain. It
contains no personal information and no identifiers.

This is written to your device's local browser storage. It never leaves the
device, is never transmitted anywhere, and is not accessible to the developer
or to anyone else.

## How to erase it

- **Reset progress** on the app's home screen erases it immediately.
- Deleting or uninstalling the app erases it.

## Data we do not collect

No name, email address, phone number, account identifier, advertising
identifier, device identifier, IP-based location, contacts, photos, health
records, or usage telemetry. Nothing is sold, shared, or disclosed, because
nothing is collected.

## Children

The app is intended for practicing nurses and nursing students. It collects
no data from anyone, including children.

## Health information

The app contains general educational content about multiple sclerosis nursing
for exam preparation. It is not a medical device, not clinical decision
support, and not medical advice. It does not store, process, or transmit any
patient information, and must not be used to record patient data.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published in this file and reflected
in the app's About screen with an updated date.
